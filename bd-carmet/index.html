/// 03 — THE PROCESS

FROM SKETCH
TO STEEL.

  1. 01

    Design & Survey

    We measure on site with laser precision and model every member in 3D before a single plate is cut.

  2. 02

    Engineering

    Static calculations, weld specifications and EN 1090 execution class — documented and approved.

  3. 03

    Fabrication

    Laser cutting, CNC bending and certified TIG/MIG welding in our workshop, under continuous quality control.

  4. 04

    Finish & Install

    Galvanising or powder coating, then assembly on site by our own mounting teams — handed over clean and on schedule.
